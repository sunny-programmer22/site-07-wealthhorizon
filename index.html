How Fast Does Money Double?

Divide 72 by your annual interest rate to find the exact number of years it takes for your investment to double without any additional deposits:

Years to Double = 72 / Return %

At an 8% S&P 500 average return, your money doubles every 9.0 years. At 10%, it doubles every 7.2 years!

Stock & Index Fund Fundamentals

Over 92% of actively managed mutual funds fail to beat the S&P 500 over a 15-year period after accounting for fees. Learn the fundamental principles that generate dependable long-term market wealth.

01 Diversification

Total Market Indexing

Instead of attempting to find the needle in the haystack, buy the entire haystack. Low-cost index funds like VOO (S&P 500) or VTI (Total US Stock) grant instant ownership in thousands of profitable businesses.

Historical Return: ~10.2% annualized nominal over the last 50 years.
02 Risk Elimination

Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)

Trying to time market bottoms is a proven losing strategy. Dollar-cost averaging invests a fixed dollar amount at regular intervals (e.g. 1st of every month), systematically buying more shares when prices dip and fewer when prices rise.

Key Rule: Time in the market always beats timing the market.
03 Wealth Protection

Minimizing Expense Ratios

A seemingly small 1.0% advisor or mutual fund management fee consumes up to 30% of your lifetime retirement wealth over 35 years due to lost compounding. Always prioritize ultra-low expense ratios (<0.05%).

Vanguard VOO fee: 0.03% ($3/yr per $10,000 invested).
